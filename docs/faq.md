# FAQ

The questions I hear most, with the quickest way out.

### Pictures stay covered

That’s often me doing my job: I cover more than I need to. To let more through:

- Move **Mia’s mood** in my popup toward **Relaxed**. Borderline pictures get through then.
- Switch off zones you don’t care about under **What I cover**.
- Some sites refuse when I load a picture a second time to check it. Those pictures stay covered, on purpose. In Firefox, some streamed videos stay blurred for the same reason.
- **Strict** hides the whole page until I’ve checked it. Switch it off if pages feel slow.

### A site looks broken

Open my popup on that site and tap **Leave this site alone**, then reload the page. If that fixes it, tell me on Discord which site it was, so I can do better.

### Adding a site I should leave alone

- **From the site:** my popup, **Leave this site alone**. Subdomains are included.
- **By hand:** **All settings**, **Sites I leave alone**, type the address, **Add**.

**Let Mia back on this site** in the popup, or **Remove** in the settings, undoes it. Banks, mail, payment pages and the like are on my fixed never-touch list already.

### The pack import fails

I always say what went wrong, and a failed import stores nothing. The usual reasons:

- **Meet Mia first:** finish my welcome steps, then import.
- **Wrong file:** pick the file ending in `.miapack`, one at a time, not a folder or a `.zip`.
- **Damaged or cut short:** download the pack again and import the fresh copy.
- **Needs a newer HYPNOMIA VEIL:** [update me](updates.md) first, then import again.

### Chrome or Edge warns about developer mode

Hand-loaded extensions run in developer mode, and the browser likes to mention it. Close the note and keep me switched on. Don’t click anything that disables extensions in developer mode, or I stop until you switch me back on in `chrome://extensions` (`edge://extensions` in Edge).

### Android: I can’t find “Install extension from file”

The entry hides until you unlock it: **Settings**, **About Firefox**, tap the Firefox logo five times quickly, then go back to **Settings** and look under **Advanced**. Android 9 and older don’t show the entry at all. Full steps: [Install on Android](install-firefox-android.md).

### Switching off my ads

**All settings**, **Mia’s control**, switch off **My ads**. Since version 0.9.0 they’re off until you answer **Yes, show them** when I ask “Show my ads?” in the **Mia Pack** section, at the top of **Set me up**, or in my popup.

### Deleting my data

- **The pack:** **Mia Pack**, **Remove Mia Pack**.
- **The counter:** **Mia’s Ledger**, **Clear my ledger**.
- **Everything at once:** [uninstall me](uninstall.md). All I stored in your browser goes with me.
