# How I work

What I do with your pages, and how you steer me. Most of it needs the [Mia Pack](mia-pack.md), every part has its own switch, and [Pause](pause.md) stops all of it at once.

## Pin me

Keep my icon where you can see it. My popup holds **Pause Mia**, and I want that one click away.

- **Chrome and Edge:** click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin next to HYPNOMIA VEIL. In Edge it may look like an eye (**Show in toolbar**).
- **Firefox on a computer:** right-click my icon, or click the extensions button (the puzzle piece) and open the gear next to HYPNOMIA VEIL. Choose **Pin to Toolbar**.
- **Firefox on Android:** there’s no toolbar icon. Open the Firefox menu (three dots), tap **Extensions**, then **HYPNOMIA VEIL**.

## My ads

Only with the Mia Pack, and only if you chose **Yes, show them** when I asked “Show my ads?”. Then I swap the ad spaces on other sites for my own little posters. Each one is labeled “Mia’s ad” and links only to my own pages. Nothing about you leaves your browser.

Had enough of them?

- **All settings**, **Mia’s control**, switch off **My ads**.
- Or tap the “Mia’s ad” label on an ad and pick **Hide my ads on this site**, **Turn my ads off** or **Pause Mia**.

Changed your mind about a site? Under **My ads** in the settings, **Sites without my ads** lists every site you hid me on. **Show my ads here again** brings me back there.

## Adult sites

Switch on **Adult-site mode** under **Adult sites**, in my popup or in **All settings**. On the adult sites I know, I swap the thumbnails for pictures of me.

- **How hot do I get?** **Tease** only hints, **Hot** shows more, **Explicit** holds nothing back. It unlocks with the Mia Pack.
- **Your own sites:** on a site I don’t know yet, type its address under **Your own sites** in the settings and click **Treat as adult site**.
- **Spot adult pages by content:** I also recognize adult pages by what’s on them. Those only get my quiz and **Reveal**, never thumbnail swaps and never my gallery.

## Full replacement

My strongest trick, a switch under **Adult sites**. With **Full replacement** on, the adult sites I know and your own sites show my gallery instead of the page. The page behind it stays silent.

Pages I only spot by their content never get my gallery. It’s only for sites you or I put on the list.

Want the real site back? Tap **Pause Mia**, or **Leave this site alone** in the gallery. **Let Mia back on this site** in my popup undoes it.

## The quiz

On adult sites I put my quiz in front of videos. A covered picture can carry a **Reveal** button too: pass my quiz, and it stays uncovered for a while, then I cover it again.

It’s one question at a time, sometimes a little task: count, type, hold still. Answer right and you go on. Answer wrong and the next question comes straight away.

Sometimes I say no to a right answer anyway, never twice in a row. **How often I refuse a right answer** under **Adult sites**, **Quiz**, sets how often.

**Pause Mia** sits at the top right of every quiz, and it’s always free.

## My grip

With the Mia Pack in, my grip tightens by itself. My popup shows it under **Adult sites**, **My grip**, from level 0 to 4.

- **Up:** a minute counts when an adult site is in the tab you’re looking at, or when I cover at least 3 new pictures in that minute. Every 10 to 20 counted minutes, I go up one level.
- **Down:** 12 hours without a counted minute bring me down one level. That clock runs on real time, browser closed or not, and my popup shows how long is left.
- **Paused:** the clock stands still and picks up where it stopped.

Each level covers more. Level 1 adds breasts, bottoms and genitals even when clothed, level 2 faces, eyes and mouths, level 3 belly, feet and armpits, clothed too. Level 4 covers every picture and video where I spot a woman, completely. My grip only adds, it never switches off what you switched on.

## Strict times

Hours you pick for me to be stricter. Set them under **All settings**, **Mia’s control**, **Strict times**, with **Add a strict time**. They need the Mia Pack. During these hours:

- my grip is one level tighter, up to level 4
- flash words come more often, and **Off** stays off
- a quiz waits before videos on every site, if **Adult-site mode** is on

My popup tells you while a strict time runs. [Pause](pause.md) works as always and lets go of everything, strict time or not.

## My little notes

Now and then I leave a short note on the page, at most one every 10 minutes. Close it with the cross, or click **Turn my notes off** when I ask whether you’ve had enough. With the keyboard, switch them off under **All settings**, **Mia’s control**, **My little notes**. [Pause](pause.md) hides them too.
