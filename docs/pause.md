# Pause

Pausing me is always allowed and never costs a thing. No quiz, no waiting, no penalty. One tap, and I let go of every page at once.

## How to pause

- **Popup:** click my icon, then **Pause Mia**.
- **Keyboard (computer):** `Alt+Shift+M`. Press it again to bring me back.
- **Settings:** **Pause Mia** sits in the bar at the top of my settings page (**All settings** in my popup) and stays there while you scroll.
- **Android:** Firefox menu (three dots), **Extensions**, **HYPNOMIA VEIL**, then **Pause Mia**. Phones have no shortcut.
- **On a page:** my quiz, my gallery and my visit notes carry their own **Pause Mia** too.

## What a pause does

- The veil comes off every open page. Pictures and videos show as the site made them.
- My ads, flash words, spiral, tab titles, pointer, notes, visits and sounds stop.
- My grip stays where it is while you’re paused. Its clock stands still and picks up where it stopped.
- Nothing is deleted. Your settings and your Mia Pack stay exactly as they are.

My popup then says: “Paused on every page. I’ll be here when you want me back.”

## Coming back

Click **Let Mia back in** in my popup or in the settings, or press `Alt+Shift+M` again. A pause has no time limit. Stay away as long as you like.

## Changing the shortcut

- **Chrome and Edge:** open `chrome://extensions/shortcuts` (or `edge://extensions/shortcuts`) and set a new key for HYPNOMIA VEIL.
- **Firefox:** **Add-ons and themes**, gear icon, **Manage Extension Shortcuts**.

## Just one site?

To keep me off a single site for good, open my popup on that site and tap **Leave this site alone**. **Let Mia back on this site** undoes it. See the [FAQ](faq.md).

Want me gone completely? That’s allowed too: [Uninstall](uninstall.md).
