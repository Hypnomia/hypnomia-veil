# Orion on iPhone (experimental)

**Experimental. Untested on a real iPhone.** Nobody has tried me in Orion on an iPhone yet. I may not start at all, or some of me may not work. No promises here, and no support beyond a friendly look on Discord.

Safari doesn’t take extensions like me. Orion is a browser for iPhone and iPad that can load extensions made for Firefox and Chrome, with limits Apple sets.

## Steps

These steps follow Orion’s own help pages. I haven’t walked them myself.

1. Install the Orion browser from the App Store.
2. In Orion, download the newest Firefox file of HYPNOMIA VEIL (it ends in `.xpi`) from [my release page](https://github.com/Hypnomia/hypnomia-veil/releases/latest) and save it to your Files.
3. Tap the menu button (three dots, bottom right) and tap **Settings**.
4. Under **Extensions**, switch on support for Firefox extensions.
5. Open the menu again and tap **Extensions**.
6. Tap **+** and choose to install from a file.
7. Pick the `.xpi` file from your Files.

## If it works

My welcome page should open: the flashing warning, the age check, then your choice to hand me the reins. If it doesn’t, open my popup from the Extensions menu and look for **Meet Mia**.

## If it doesn’t

Remove me in the same **Extensions** list, then delete the downloaded file from your Files if you like.

Tried it? Tell me on [Discord](https://discord.gg/2C8YAwDkat) what happened, good or bad. That’s how this page stops being experimental.

Safe use still applies here: read [Safety](safety.md) first.
