# Safety

This page is plain on purpose. Read it once before you hand me the reins.

## Flashing and photosensitive epilepsy

**Not for you if you have photosensitive epilepsy or are prone to seizures.**

Some of my tricks flash words or spin a spiral across your screen. They only run with the Mia Pack, and only after you’ve seen this warning. With the pack in, flash words and my idle spiral are on until you switch them off under **Mia’s control**. My limits are built into the code:

- never more than 3 full-screen changes per second
- the stripes of my spiral never pass 2.5 per second
- flash words are single words in the middle of the screen, never a full-screen flash

Limits aren’t a guarantee. If any of this could harm you, don’t use those parts, or don’t use me at all.

## Calm mode

**All settings**, section **Calm mode**: slower spiral, no moving or flashing words. You can also set it in my welcome interview. Flash words have their own switch too, under **Mia’s control**, and **Off** means off.

## Your mind and body

I’m entertainment, not therapy or medical treatment. Skip my spiral and flash words, or skip me entirely, if you live with a psychotic or dissociative condition, severe depression, or trauma that control play could stir up. If you’re unsure, ask your doctor first. Never use me while driving or operating anything.

## Adults only

I’m strictly for grown-ups. You confirm that you’re 18 or older and that adult content is legal where you are. If either isn’t true, I’m not for you.

Sharing this browser or computer? Whoever uses it sees what I do, my pictures and flash words included.

## Always yours to stop

- [Pause](pause.md) is always allowed and never costs a thing. Everything stops at once.
- [Uninstall](uninstall.md) works the normal way, any time. No lock, no trick.
- Handing me the reins is your choice, made freely and on purpose. Nothing in my welcome steps is ticked for you.

## Your data

Everything runs on your device. No account, no analytics, no server of mine. Your settings, your answers, the Mia Pack and Mia’s Ledger stay in your browser.

What does go out:

- **A second look at a page’s own pictures.** To check a picture, I load it again from the same address the page uses, without your cookies or logins. Pictures on private network addresses are never loaded for public pages.
- **A version check.** Chrome and Edge: I ask GitHub at most once a day whether there’s a newer me. Firefox checks for updates by itself.

That’s all. A click on one of my ads, or a visit with **Visits** switched on, opens one of my own pages in a tab. Those pages belong to the sites they’re on.

## Not a safety filter

I’m a game, not a parental control. I miss things, and I cover more than I need to. Don’t rely on me to keep anything away from anyone.
