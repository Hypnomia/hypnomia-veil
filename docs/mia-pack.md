## Importing it

1. Finish my welcome steps first. Until then the import says **Meet Mia first**.
2. Open **All settings** and go to **Mia Pack** (my popup’s **Get the Mia Pack** takes you there). Under **Already have the file?**, you’ll find the file picker.
3. Click **Choose the pack file** and pick the `.miapack` file. On a computer you can also drop the file onto that spot.
4. I check every file inside. Under **Before it goes in** you see the title, the version and my license.
5. Read the license, then click **Accept and add**. **Not yet** stops here, and nothing is stored.
6. “There it is. The pack is in, and it never left your browser.” Your cover style is now “My face, black, words”. Face is on under What I cover, so every face becomes mine. You can switch it off again.

## After the import: my ads

New in version 0.9.0: my ads don’t switch on by themselves. Right after the import I ask you once, in the **Mia Pack** section, at the top of **Set me up**, and in my popup until you answer: “Show my ads?” Answer **Yes, show them** or **No ads**. Until you answer, they stay off. You can change your mind any time under **Mia’s control**, switch **My ads**.

My ads replace ad spaces on web pages, are always labeled “Mia’s ad”, and only ever link to my own pages.
