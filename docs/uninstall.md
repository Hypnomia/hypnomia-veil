# Uninstall

Uninstalling works the normal way, any time. No lock, no trick, no last word from me. You don’t need to pause me first.

## Chrome and Edge

1. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `edge://extensions` in Edge).
2. Find HYPNOMIA VEIL and click **Remove**.
3. Confirm with **Remove**.
4. If you like, delete the folder you loaded me from. Your browser doesn’t need it any more.

## Firefox on a computer

1. Open the menu (three lines, top right) and choose **Add-ons and themes**.
2. Find HYPNOMIA VEIL, click the three dots next to it and choose **Remove**.
3. Confirm with **Remove**.

## Firefox on Android

1. Open the Firefox menu (three dots), tap **Extensions**, then **Manage extensions**.
2. Tap HYPNOMIA VEIL, then **Remove**.

## Orion on iPhone

See [Orion on iPhone](install-orion-ios.md): remove me in Orion’s **Extensions** list.

## Afterwards

Reload the tabs that were open. A page can keep my veil or my cover until it loads fresh.

## What is deleted

Everything I stored inside your browser goes with me:

- your settings and your answers from the welcome interview
- the imported Mia Pack
- your list of sites I leave alone and your strict times
- Mia’s Ledger, my little counter

## What stays

- The Mia Pack file you downloaded. It sits in your Downloads or wherever you put it. Keep it to import again later, or delete it.
- On Chrome and Edge, the folder you loaded me from, until you delete it.
- Nothing of mine stays anywhere else. There’s no account and no server that knows you.

## Coming back later

Install me again like the first time. You walk through my welcome steps again and import the pack file again. See [README](README.md).

Only want me gone for a while? [Pause](pause.md) does that without losing anything.
