# Install me in Firefox

Firefox on a computer, version 128 or newer. My Firefox file is signed by Mozilla, so Firefox accepts it without tricks, and it keeps me up to date by itself.

## Steps

1. Download the newest Firefox file of HYPNOMIA VEIL (it ends in `.xpi`) from [my release page](https://github.com/Hypnomia/hypnomia-veil/releases/latest).
2. If Firefox asks right away whether to add HYPNOMIA VEIL, click **Add** and skip to step 6.
3. Otherwise open the menu (three lines, top right) and choose **Add-ons and themes**.
4. Click the gear icon next to “Manage Your Extensions” and choose **Install Add-on From File**.
5. Pick the `.xpi` file you downloaded.
6. Firefox asks whether to add HYPNOMIA VEIL. Click **Add**.
7. Pin me to the toolbar when Firefox offers it, so my popup is one click away.

Firefox asks for access to your data on all websites. I need it to put my veil over pictures on every page. Nothing about you leaves your browser. See [Safety](safety.md).

## What happens next

My welcome page opens by itself: the flashing warning, the age check, then your choice to hand me the reins. Nothing is ticked for you.

If it doesn’t open, click my icon and choose **Meet Mia**.

The veil starts right away. The [Mia Pack](mia-pack.md) waits until the welcome steps are done.

Keep my icon pinned to the toolbar, so **Pause Mia** is always one click away: [Pin me](how-i-work.md#pin-me).

## Good to know

- Firefox updates me by itself. Nothing for you to do. See [Updates](updates.md).
- Pausing me is always free: **Pause Mia** in my popup, or `Alt+Shift+M`. See [Pause](pause.md).
- My settings open with **All settings** in my popup, or in **Add-ons and themes**: HYPNOMIA VEIL, tab **Preferences**.
